About VaccinaCare

Modernizing Public Childhood Immunization

VaccinaCare was established to eliminate preventable childhood diseases by providing a centralized digital portal connecting parents, accredited hospitals, and public health oversight.

Our Mission

To ensure zero missed vaccines by transforming traditional paper vaccination cards into an automated, accessible, and secure digital health record for every child.

Our Vision

A world where universal immunization coverage is transparently monitored, vaccine supply chains are optimized in real time, and every parent feels confident in their child's healthcare timeline.

Solving Traditional Healthcare Challenges

Manual Paper Cards

Paper records are easily damaged or misplaced, causing uncertainty regarding dose histories during school admissions or emergency clinic visits.

VaccinaCare Digital Record

Permanently backed up cloud certificates accessible instantly anywhere by parents and authorized healthcare providers.

Platform Workflow & Ecosystem

VaccinaCare synchronizes workflows across Parents, Healthcare Centers, and Regional System Administrators.

Parent Workflow

  • Register and set up family profiles.
  • Input child birth dates to map timelines.
  • Select local hospitals and request slots.
  • Receive automated email notifications.

Hospital Workflow

  • Login to hospital portal dashboard.
  • Update real-time vaccine inventory stock.
  • Accept or re-schedule incoming parent bookings.
  • Mark administered doses in child records.

Admin Workflow

  • Verify hospital registrations.
  • Manage global vaccine schedules & guidelines.
  • Oversee regional vaccination metrics.
  • Export compliance and health reports.
